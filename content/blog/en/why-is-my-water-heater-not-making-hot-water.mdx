**Start by checking whether the problem affects one fixture or the whole home.** That distinction is more useful than assuming the water heater has failed. A shower mixing problem can feel similar to a heater problem while needing a different repair.

## Is every hot-water tap affected?

Carefully check another accessible tap, allowing for the normal wait in your home. Do not test water with a hand under a stream that may suddenly become hot. Note whether water is completely cold, briefly warm or simply runs out earlier than usual.

Record recent heavy use, a power interruption, plumbing work and any visible error code. Photograph the model label if it is accessible without removing covers. A code is a clue to report, not a reason to repeatedly reset the appliance.

## What should I leave to a professional?

Do not open electrical compartments, adjust gas components or relight equipment without following its exact manufacturer instructions. If you smell gas, leave the area and contact the gas utility or emergency services from a safe place. A leaking tank or water near electrical parts needs prompt attention.

[A. O. Smith’s no-hot-water overview](https://www.hotwater.com/info-center/water-heater-not-working.html) illustrates why causes differ by heater type. Your own model’s instructions remain the relevant reference for operation.

## Does this mean I need a replacement?

Not necessarily. Ask the plumber to identify the fault, whether the tank itself is sound and whether a repair is supported by the appliance’s condition. If replacement is indicated, [Supremacy’s water-heater replacement page](https://supremacyservice.com/water-heater-replacement-and-installation-services/) is the appropriate service link.

A replacement quote should explain the proposed equipment, installation changes and disposal. If someone suggests switching technologies, use [Supremacy’s tank-versus-tankless comparison](https://supremacyservice.com/tank-vs-tankless-water-heater-dallas/) to prepare questions about household demand and the existing connections rather than assuming every outage calls for tankless.

### Why is only the shower cold?

The fixture or mixing valve may be involved. Report that other taps still receive hot water so the plumber can narrow the inspection.

### Should I keep resetting the heater?

No. Repeated faults deserve diagnosis. Record the displayed message and arrange qualified service instead of bypassing a safety shutdown.
