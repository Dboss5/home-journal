**Killing the roaches you see may leave the underlying activity untouched.** The species, location of sightings, and earlier treatment all matter when deciding what to do next.

## What should I change first?

Keep food sealed and rubbish contained. Note moisture and clutter around the places where you see activity. The [University of California's cockroach guidance](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/cockroaches/) explains why sanitation and exclusion matter alongside treatment; simply applying more spray is not a complete plan.

Photograph a roach safely and record the room and time. Do not spray into electrical outlets or improvise pesticide mixtures. Keep products away from children and pets and follow their labels.
