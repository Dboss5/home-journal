**Water may be escaping past the tank seal, or the fill mechanism may not be shutting off correctly.** The sound alone cannot identify which part needs attention.

## What should I notice?

Record whether it runs continuously, restarts between flushes, or stops when the handle returns to its normal position. Check for water on the floor without dismantling fittings.

The [EPA's toilet maintenance guidance](https://www.epa.gov/watersense/home-maintenance) recommends investigating constant running and water flowing into the overflow tube. Share those observations when discussing [toilet repairs and rebuilds with Supremacy Service](https://supremacyservice.com/toilet-repairs-and-rebuilds/); ask which repair fits your toilet model before buying a replacement kit.

## Can I leave it until later?

Persistent running can waste water, so arrange attention rather than ignoring it. If water is overflowing, avoid flushing again and use the fixture shutoff only if you know how and can operate it safely. Do not force a seized valve.

If the toilet is repaired but unexplained water use continues, [Supremacy Service's leak detection service](https://supremacyservice.com/water-leak-detection/) is a relevant next step. Ask whether another fixture or supply leak needs investigation.
