Plumbing costs are notoriously hard to pin down. Every provider wants to send someone out before quoting a price. That's frustrating, especially when the issue is straightforward — a dripping faucet, a running toilet, a slow drain.

This guide gives you real DFW numbers up front. Rates current as of early 2025.

## The short answer

**Standard plumbing service calls in DFW run $75–$150** just to show up. **Common repairs cost $150–$500 total.** Bigger jobs — water heater replacement, repiping, slab leaks — range from $800 to $15,000+ depending on scope.

## What drives the price

**Accessibility.** A repair under the sink is easy. A repair behind a wall or under a slab isn't.

**Time of day.** Emergency after-hours calls cost 50–100% more than weekday daytime.

**Parts.** Standard fixtures are cheap; specialty or imported ones aren't.

**Permits.** Major work requires permits. A good plumber includes them in the quote.

**Age of home.** Older homes have older pipes. Repairs are more invasive and costlier.

## What to look for in a plumber

Good signs:

- State-licensed (Texas requires a master plumber license for most work)
- Bonded and insured
- Flat-rate or upfront pricing
- Written estimate before work begins
- Warranty on parts and labor
- Clean, marked vehicle

Red flags:

- Won't give a written quote
- Wants payment in cash only
- No license number on the truck or invoice
- Prices "start at" without specifics
- Tries to upsell unrelated work

## How to get a fair quote

Ask:

1. What's the service call fee, and does it apply toward the repair?
2. Is the estimate written and firm?
3. Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?
4. What's the warranty on parts and labor?
5. Are permits included?

Good plumbers answer all five clearly. Bad ones dodge.
