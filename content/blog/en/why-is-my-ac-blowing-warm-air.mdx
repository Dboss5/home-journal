**Warm air does not automatically mean low refrigerant.** Thermostat settings, restricted airflow, or a fault needing diagnosis can produce similar complaints.

## What can I check first?

Confirm cooling mode and a temperature below the room reading. Check the filter according to its instructions and keep return grilles clear. [ENERGY STAR explains how a dirty filter restricts airflow](https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome/heating-cooling); replacing it does not rule out another fault.

Note whether every room is affected and whether the outdoor unit appears to run, without opening equipment. [Supremacy Service's AC-not-cooling guide](https://supremacyservice.com/ac-not-cooling-in-dallas-9-common-causes/) covers relevant symptoms to describe when booking.

## When should I call?

Arrange [AC repair with Supremacy Service](https://supremacyservice.com/air-conditioner-repair/) if warm air persists or the unit repeatedly stops. Report error codes, unusual sounds, and when the problem began. If you see ice, stop cooling and ask for guidance; do not chip it off or add refrigerant yourself.

Smoke, sparking, or a burning electrical smell means stop using the system and seek urgent help. Ask for a diagnosis before approving parts or a refrigerant recharge.
