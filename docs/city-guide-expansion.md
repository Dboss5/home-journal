# Expanded city service guides

## Coverage

The six existing cities (Dallas, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Fort Worth and Arlington) across seven existing services produce 42 city/service combinations, with English, Spanish and French versions (126 routes). No new city routes were invented.

Add local profiles, 42 distinct service/city planning notes with translations, property-access considerations, scope and quote guidance, visit preparation and relevant article links. City reference points provide orientation rather than guaranteed neighborhood coverage. Official/local resources are linked in the visible profile sections. Requirements and availability remain property-specific.

Replace unsupported citywide price ranges, population badges and rigid repair/treatment rules with dated-estimate guidance. Seven native-details FAQ answers are present in server-rendered HTML and match the FAQ structured data. Service and provider separation remains Poolburg for pools and Supremacy Service for the other existing categories.

## Visuals

Each city has a different local landmark photograph plus a service-photo inset. Add responsive content cards, local notes, jump navigation, a quote question sheet, numbered preparation steps and a lazy OpenStreetMap city reference. The map marker identifies the existing city reference coordinates, not an office, customer address or verified service boundary. OpenStreetMap attribution and a larger-map link remain visible.

The six downloaded photographs were visually inspected before use. Images are resized and re-encoded as WebP, with visible artist, source, license and adaptation credits. The licenses apply to their respective images, including ShareAlike terms where applicable, not to an assertion that an image is a Homefront customer project.

## SEO and booking

Preserve canonical and en/es/fr/x-default language alternates, add unique localized descriptions and share the actual city photograph in social metadata. Remove the legacy English-only generated social card containing unsupported pricing claims. City structured data now includes linked WebPage/Service data, a four-level breadcrumb and FAQ data matching the seven rendered answers. No branch addresses, ratings, reviews, universal price claims or search ranking promises are added.

Correct city quote links so pest-control passes service=pest and holiday-lighting passes service=lighting, with the city retained.

- All city URLs remain in the sitemap. Relevant article targets exist in the corresponding language.
